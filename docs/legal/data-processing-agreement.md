# Data Processing Agreement

Effective date: 26 June 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service or other written agreement between the customer and `[legal company name]` trading as KSeF Helper.

This DPA is a template and must be reviewed before launch.

## 1. Roles

For customer content uploaded to or generated in the service, the customer is the controller and KSeF Helper is the processor.

Customer content includes invoice XML files, parsed invoice data, company records, validation results, exports, organization content, and related metadata.

For account administration, billing, security, service operations, legal compliance, and support records, KSeF Helper may act as an independent controller as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 2. Processing instructions

KSeF Helper will process customer content only:

- to provide, secure, monitor, support, improve, export, and delete the service;
- according to the customer's configuration and documented instructions;
- as required by applicable law.

The Terms, product settings, support requests, and this DPA are the customer's documented instructions.

KSeF Helper must promptly inform the customer if it believes an instruction infringes applicable data protection law, unless prohibited by law.

## 3. Processing details

Subject matter:

- hosted invoice XML validation, preview, archive, export, deletion, audit, and organization management.

Duration:

- for the subscription term and any retention/deletion period described in the Retention and Deletion Policy.

Nature and purpose:

- storage, parsing, validation, preview, search, export, backup, logging, security monitoring, support, deletion, and audit of uploaded invoice and organization data.

Categories of personal data:

- names, emails, business contact details, tax identifiers, addresses, invoice numbers, invoice dates, bank account data, line items, amounts, VAT data, organization roles, account identifiers, IP addresses, user agents, audit events, and any other personal data contained in uploaded XML or organization records.

Categories of data subjects:

- customer users;
- customer's staff and contractors;
- sellers, buyers, invoice contacts, representatives, and other persons appearing in uploaded invoices;
- support contacts.

## 4. Confidentiality

KSeF Helper will ensure that personnel authorized to process customer content are bound by confidentiality obligations and process customer content only as needed for the service.

## 5. Security measures

KSeF Helper will maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures, including where applicable:

- access control and authentication;
- organization-level authorization boundaries;
- least-privilege production database roles;
- encrypted object storage in production;
- password hashing;
- rotating refresh tokens;
- rate limiting;
- XML parser hardening and validator resource limits;
- audit logging;
- backup and restore procedures;
- monitoring, readiness checks, and error tracking;
- deletion queues and dead-letter handling;
- incident response procedures.

## 6. Subprocessors

The customer authorizes KSeF Helper to use subprocessors needed to provide the service.

Before public launch, KSeF Helper must publish an actual subprocessor list in the Privacy Policy or a dedicated subprocessor page.

KSeF Helper remains responsible for subprocessor performance to the extent required by applicable data protection law.

KSeF Helper should give customers reasonable notice before adding or replacing material subprocessors, unless urgent replacement is needed for security, availability, or legal reasons.

## 7. Data subject requests

KSeF Helper will assist the customer, using appropriate technical and organizational measures, with data subject requests concerning customer content.

The customer remains responsible for deciding how to respond to such requests.

## 8. Security incidents and personal data breaches

KSeF Helper will notify affected customers without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting customer content.

The notice should include, where available:

- incident description;
- affected data categories;
- likely consequences;
- mitigation steps;
- contact point for follow-up.

KSeF Helper will cooperate reasonably with customer breach assessment and notification duties.

## 9. Deletion and return

During the subscription, customers may export organization data using service export features.

After termination or deletion, KSeF Helper will delete or anonymize customer content according to the Retention and Deletion Policy, unless Union, Member State, or other applicable law requires retention.

Backups are deleted through normal backup rotation and are protected from ordinary access.

## 10. Audits

KSeF Helper will provide reasonable information needed to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, such as security documentation, subprocessors, retention settings, and audit summaries.

On-site audits require advance notice, confidentiality, reasonable scope, and no disruption to other customers or production systems.

## 11. International transfers

KSeF Helper will not transfer customer content outside the EEA unless a valid transfer mechanism applies, such as adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, or another lawful safeguard.

Actual hosting and subprocessor locations must be documented before launch.

## 12. Liability and conflict

Liability is governed by the main commercial agreement unless applicable law requires otherwise.

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms regarding personal data processing, this DPA controls for that processing issue.

## Appendix A: Minimum production commitments

Before public paid launch, KSeF Helper must complete:

- actual operator identity and contact details;
- actual subprocessor list;
- actual hosting region and transfer mechanism;
- tested backup restore procedure;
- tested account and organization deletion procedure;
- production incident contact;
- security review of authentication, storage, audit, export, and retention controls.

## Support scope

Support covers:

- account access problems;
- email verification and password reset issues;
- organization and role problems;
- invoice upload errors;
- validation result questions about how the software works;
- export and deletion requests;
- suspected bugs;
- security reports.

Support does not include:

- tax advice;
- legal advice;
- accounting advice;
- official KSeF certification;
- deciding whether an invoice should be submitted to KSeF;
- filing, sending, or correcting tax records on the customer's behalf.

## Security reports

Report suspected vulnerabilities or unauthorized access to `[security email]`.

Please include:

- affected URL or endpoint;
- steps to reproduce;
- impact;
- screenshots or logs where safe;
- contact details for follow-up.

Do not access, modify, delete, export, or disclose another customer's data.

## Product disclaimer

KSeF Helper is not an official KSeF, Ministry of Finance, government, tax, legal, accounting, or certification tool.

The service helps validate, preview, archive, export, and delete FA(3) XML invoice data, but customers remain responsible for legal, tax, accounting, and KSeF obligations.
